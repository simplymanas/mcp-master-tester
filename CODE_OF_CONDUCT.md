# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MCP Master Tester to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Helping newcomers get started

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **simplymanas@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
